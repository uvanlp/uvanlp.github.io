The Information and Language Processing (ILP) Lab at the University of Virginia is led by [Yangfeng Ji](https://yangfengji.net) and is part of the [Computer Science Department](https://engineering.virginia.edu/departments/computer-science).

We bring together people and ideas to advance research in natural language processing.
